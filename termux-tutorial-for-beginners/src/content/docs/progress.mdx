This is your personal dashboard. Set a display name and avatar, and check off
lessons as you finish them.

:::note[Everything here is local]
Your profile and progress live in **this browser's local storage** — there's no
account and nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data (or using a
different device or private window) starts you fresh.
:::
