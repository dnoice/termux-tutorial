<p class="tmx-lede">
  Most tutorials hand you a wall of commands and wish you luck. This one hands
  you a <strong class="tmx-gradient-text">live terminal</strong> and stands next
  to you while you use it. Read a little, type a little, break things safely —
  that's the whole deal.
</p>

Go ahead — poke it. Type `pkg update`, then `termux-setup-storage`. The grey
text ahead of your cursor is the terminal guessing what you meant; tap
<kbd class="tmx-kbd">→</kbd> or <kbd class="tmx-kbd">Tab</kbd> to accept it.
Reading this on a phone, where your keyboard has neither key? Just type the
command out — and note the problem, because solving it on your real device is
[a lesson of its own](/start/sessions-and-copy-paste/).

That guessing comes from a shell called **fish**. You'll install it in lesson
four; this practice terminal just has it switched on from the start.

<TermuxTerminal client:only="react" hint="Lost? Type 'help' — it lists everything this practice terminal knows." />

:::tip[Before you start]
**You need nothing at all to begin.** Nearly every lesson has a terminal built
in, so you can read and practise the course right here and install Termux later
— that's the point of the thing above.

**To follow along on your phone:** Android 7 or newer, about **200 MB** free,
and **Wi-Fi** for the first package update (it pulls tens of megabytes).

**You do not need:** root, a computer, a rooted bootloader, or money.

**Time:** about 90 minutes end to end, and it saves your place as you go.
:::
