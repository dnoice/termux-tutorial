This course assumes you finished the beginner course, or already live in a
terminal. `pkg`, `~/storage`, fish, sessions and the extra-keys row are treated
as things you know. Nothing here needs root, a computer, or money.

<TermuxTerminal client:only="react" hint="Warm up: pkg search termux — the package this whole course runs on is in there." />

:::tip[Before you start]
**Bring a real phone this time.** The practice terminal above is still here for
muscle memory, but every `termux-*` command in this course talks to actual
Android hardware — there is no simulating a battery or a camera.

**You need:** Termux installed from **F-Droid** (not the Play Store), Android 7
or newer, about **300 MB** free, and Wi-Fi.

**You will also install:** the **Termux:API** companion app, from the same
source as Termux itself. Lesson one explains why "same source" is not optional.

**You do not need:** root, a computer, or a rooted bootloader.

**Time:** about 3 hours end to end, and it saves your place as you go.
:::
